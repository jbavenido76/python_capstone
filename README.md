## 📌 Project Overview

The **Shop at Fingertips Sales Performance Dashboard** is a Python-based Business Intelligence project designed to analyze sales performance, profitability, product contribution, regional performance, and customer purchasing behavior.

The project transforms raw sales transaction data into an interactive dashboard using Python and Streamlit, allowing business stakeholders to monitor key performance indicators (KPIs), identify trends, and make data-driven business decisions.

The dashboard provides an executive-level overview of business performance through **6 KPIs and 8 interactive visualizations**.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Monitor overall revenue and sales performance.
- Evaluate gross profit and profitability trends.
- Identify top-performing products and categories.
- Analyze regional revenue contributions.
- Understand customer purchasing behavior.
- Monitor order cancellation trends.
- Evaluate revenue contribution by sales channel.
- Generate actionable business insights and recommendations.

## 🔍 Key Business Insights

1. **Revenue Performance:** The business generated approximately ₱2.31 million in revenue from 5,332 orders.

2. **Profitability:** Gross profit reached approximately ₱866.53K, with a gross profit margin of 37.44%.

3. **Customer Retention:** The repeat customer rate of 43.45% indicates opportunities to strengthen customer loyalty.

4. **Regional Performance:** Central Visayas emerged as the leading revenue-generating region.

5. **Product Performance:** The top 10 products provide important opportunities for inventory prioritization and promotional campaigns.

6. **Category Profitability:** Toys, Electronics, and Fashion are among the leading categories in gross profit contribution.

7. **Customer Spending:** The average order value of ₱434.02 provides a baseline for improving customer transaction value.

8. **Data Monitoring:** The sharp decline in revenue and cancellations during the later reporting period requires further validation to determine whether it reflects actual business activity or incomplete data.
